Neural Battle

Two mixers, one dataset, online. Each game draws two characters from 0–9 and A–Z; each player tunes 16 weights of their own logistic regression (bias fixed at 0) to tell them apart. When the clock runs out, 6 unseen test images decide the winner: lowest test loss takes it.
